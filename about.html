About Me

I am an Associate Professor of Physics at York University, where I work across physics, astronomy, education, and public engagement with science.

I am particularly interested in helping people understand science—not simply by giving them answers, but by helping them develop the tools and confidence to find answers themselves.

Physics & Astronomy

My work is rooted in physics and astronomy and in the broader question of how we understand the universe. I am also interested in finding ways to make astronomy and physics accessible and engaging to people beyond the university classroom.

Teaching & Engagement

My approach to teaching emphasizes curiosity, critical thinking, and resilience. I want students to leave a course with more than a collection of facts and equations—they should have a better sense of how to approach unfamiliar problems and questions.
