## Teaching
Most of my teaching is in the Division of Natural Science, which offers large introductory science courses to students outside the Faculty of Science as part of their general education requirements. My students are pursuing degrees that lead to every kind of career, so my courses aim not only to teach science but to show how science fits into society, and to build students' lasting engagement with it.

* C4: Cross-Campus Capstone Classroom (2022–2024)
* PHYS 5490 3.0: Astronomical Research Project (2022–2023)
* NATS 1530 3.0: Science of Space Flight (2021–2023)
* NATS 1570 3.0: Exploring the Solar System (2020–2023)
* NATS 1740 6.0: Astronomy (2021–2026)
* NATS 1745 6.0: History of Astronomy (2020–2026)

Since 2020 I have taught more than 4,500 students at York. I lead a team of teaching assistants in each course and run a teaching mentorship program for graduate students who want to try teaching and get feedback.

## Research
My research focuses on quasars, actively feeding supermassive black holes in the distant universe, and in particular on the variability of broad absorption line outflows.
